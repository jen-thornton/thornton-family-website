About

More than the
sum of her notes

Natalie just wrapped up four years at Cairine Wilson Secondary School — years spent playing clarinet in two bands, mentoring Grade 9 students through a leadership course, organising concerts, running bake sales, and somehow still finding time to fall deeper in love with mathematics.

She's the kind of person who doesn't just participate — she helps make things happen. Whether that's arranging a video game piece for the year-end concert or making sure the ticket booth runs smoothly, she's learned that good organisation and genuine care for the people around you goes a long way.

This September she begins her Honours Bachelor of Mathematics at the University of Ottawa, ready to bring that same energy to a whole new chapter.

"Curiosity got me into mathematics. Clarinet taught me how to practise. Four years of school events taught me everything else."

— Natalie Thornton

Highlights

Things worth
being proud of

Music · 2026 Ottawa Musicfest

Silver Medal — CW Concert Band

Member of the Cairine Wilson Concert Band that earned a silver placement at the 2026 Ottawa Musicfest. Competing at a festival alongside bands from across the region, the result was a testament to the group's commitment through a full year of rehearsal.

Music · Year-End Concert

Arranged "BB's Song" for Graduate Performance

For the CW year-end concert, Natalie arranged BB's Song from the video game Death Stranding as the graduate performance piece — bringing a piece of music she loved into a format the full band could perform together as a send-off.

Jazz Band

Clarinetist, CW Jazz & Concert Bands

Four years of active membership in both the CW Concert Band and Jazz Band — performing across a wide range of styles and repertoire, and developing the discipline and ensemble awareness that only comes from playing alongside others week after week.

Music · Selected · Two Years Running

OCDSB All-Star Band

Hand-selected twice to join the OCDSB All-Star Band — an ensemble that draws top student musicians from across Ottawa-area high schools and prepares a full concert in roughly seven rehearsals. Being chosen once is an honour; being chosen again says something about the consistency behind it.

Leadership · Grade 12

Link Crew — Mentoring Grade 9 Students

Completed Link, a Grade 12 leadership course built around supporting incoming Grade 9 students as they transition into high school. Natalie served as a mentor, helping new students navigate routines, find their footing, and feel genuinely welcome from day one.

Event Organisation

Band Concerts & Bake Sale Coordinator

Played an active role organising multiple band concerts and events — from logistics and promotion through to ticket sales and running bake sales. The kind of work that's invisible when it goes right and only noticed when it doesn't.

Academia · Accepted 2026

Honours Bachelor of Mathematics, uOttawa

Accepted into the Honours Bachelor of Mathematics programme at the University of Ottawa, beginning September 2026 — on the strength of an 86% graduating average. A natural next step for someone who has always found as much satisfaction in an elegant proof as in a well-played phrase.

Languages · OCDSB FSL Programme

DELF B2 — Certified Bilingual, English & French

Holder of the DELF B2 certification, awarded by the French Ministry of Education and recognised internationally as proof of upper-intermediate French proficiency. B2 means Natalie can communicate fluently and spontaneously — reading, writing, listening, and speaking — with real confidence in both official languages.

What I bring

Skills built in
the real world

Organisation & Event Planning

Planned and delivered multiple school events from the ground up — coordinating logistics, managing ticket sales, handling promotion, and keeping things moving on the day. Good at seeing what needs doing before being asked.

Mentorship & Peer Support

Genuinely enjoys helping people find their feet. Through Link and the wider school community, Natalie has practised meeting people where they are — patient, encouraging, and good at making others feel like they belong.

Analytical Thinking

A mathematical mind that enjoys working through problems carefully and logically. Comfortable sitting with a hard question long enough to actually understand it rather than reaching for the nearest shortcut.

Creativity & Arrangement

Arranging BB's Song for a full concert band from scratch required listening, musical intuition, and a lot of patience with notation software. Natalie approaches creative problems with the same methodical curiosity she brings to mathematics.

Teamwork & Ensemble Skills

Years of band have made listening a reflex. Playing in an ensemble means constant awareness of the people around you — when to lead, when to follow, and how to make the whole sound better than the parts.

Reliability & Follow-through

The kind of person who signs up for something and then actually does it. Bake sales get baked for. Concerts get organised. New students get met at the door. Natalie takes commitments seriously and sees them through.
