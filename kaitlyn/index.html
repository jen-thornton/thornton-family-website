About

Creative at heart,
curious about everything

Kaitlyn just wrapped up Grade 9 at Cairine Wilson Secondary School — a year that took her from the art room to the canoe, from the snowshoe trail to the orienteering course. One of the genuine highlights was Outdoor Education, where she got to experience adventures that most people only read about: snowshoeing, orienteering, cycling, and canoeing.

Art has always been at the centre of things for Kaitlyn. She's a multimedia artist with a real passion for drawing and animation, and has taken several extracurricular art classes to keep developing her craft. Whether she's sketching characters, experimenting with animation, or illustrating something just for the fun of it, there's always something in progress.

She's a fun-loving, kind, and deeply empathetic person — someone who genuinely cares about the people around her. She loves hanging out with her besties, gaming together, and marathoning medical dramas (a passion that, it turns out, is pointing her toward a future in the health sciences — possibly cardiology or medical imaging).

"I want to do something that actually helps people — and I think health sciences is where art and care come together for me."

— Kaitlyn Thornton
